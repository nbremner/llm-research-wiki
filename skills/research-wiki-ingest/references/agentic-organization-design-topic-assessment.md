# Topic assessment — agentic-organization-design (behavioral-science boundary)

Owner ruling 2026-09-08 (rejection of [[2026-gao-making-agent-mediated-contributions-governable]]).

**What the topic is for.** `agentic-organization-design` synthesizes how multi-agent topology, shared context, traces, validation, permissions, and accountability interfaces determine whether agent collectives produce usable collective intelligence — as an *organizational and behavioral* question: who is accountable, how humans steer and verify, what work and coordination look like when agents are participants.

**What does not belong, even when it names the topic.** A source whose contribution is exclusively the technical implementation of AI agents or agent systems — architectures, protocols, governance-as-code manifests, evaluation harnesses, engineering practice — with no behavioral, organizational, or work-design evidence, theory, or framework about people and organizations. Relevance to the topic's *subject* is not enough; the wiki's lens is I-O / organizational science. Such papers are read-once at triage, and a batch that receives one anyway should propose rejection rather than integrate it.

**Reusable test.** Ask: does the paper make or test a claim about workers, teams, organizations, roles, accountability, or design choices that people make? If every claim is about the system's mechanics, it is out of scope here (and probably everywhere in this wiki).

**Refinement — owner ruling 2026-10-12 (acceptance of [[2026-sapkota-ai-agents-vs-agentic-ai]]).** The boundary is about *claims*, not subject matter. A conceptual taxonomy of AI-agent work forms — tool/task-oriented agents versus coordinated multi-agent "agentic" systems with dynamic task decomposition and persistent memory — stays in the wiki when it can be read as a parallel to how human work is organized: "a helpful agentic work taxonomy that could be a potentially useful parallel to human work." Synthesize such a source for what its distinctions imply about roles, decomposition, coordination and accountability in human-agent work (candidate pages: `agentic-organization-design`, `work-redesign`), not for its architectures or engineering remedies. Implementation-only papers (the Gao case above) remain read-once, and a batch must not re-propose rejection of a source the owner has ruled in.
