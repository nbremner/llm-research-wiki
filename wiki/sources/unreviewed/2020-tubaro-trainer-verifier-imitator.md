# The trainer, the verifier, the imitator: Three ways in which human platform workers support artificial intelligence

**Citation.** Tubaro, P., Casilli, A. A., & Coville, M. (2020). *The trainer, the verifier, the imitator: Three ways in which human platform workers support artificial intelligence*. *Big Data & Society*.

**Summary.** Drawing on desk research, an online questionnaire, and interviews, this qualitative study argues that platform micro-work is a structural component of data-intensive AI production rather than a temporary residue of incomplete automation.

## Key claims
- Platform micro-workers support AI through preparation (data generation and annotation), verification (checking and correcting outputs), and impersonation (performing tasks presented as automated or beyond current automation).
- The three-function typology locates human labor both before and after model outputs, challenging accounts that treat AI production as independent of ongoing human work.
- The authors link the hidden and fragmented organization of micro-work to risks of marginalization and precarity rather than assuming automation simply eliminates labor.

## Evidence & limitations
- The paper synthesizes desk research with an online questionnaire and in-depth interviews; its qualitative, exploratory evidence supports a functional typology but does not estimate the prevalence or causal effects of each form of micro-work.
