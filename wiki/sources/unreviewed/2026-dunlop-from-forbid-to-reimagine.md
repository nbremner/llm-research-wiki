# From Forbid to Reimagine: Employer Strategies for Responding to Candidate GenAI Use in Assessment

**Citation.** Dunlop, P. D., & Lievens, F. (2026). From forbid to reimagine: Employer strategies for responding to candidate GenAI use in assessment. *International Journal of Selection and Assessment, 34*(2), e70060. https://doi.org/10.1111/ijsa.70060

**Summary.** This peer-reviewed commentary responds to prior discussion of candidate GenAI use in personnel selection and proposes the FAIR framework: Forbid, Advise, Insulate, and Reimagine. It argues that emerging agentic AI and AI-integrated wearables increase opportunities for GenAI to substitute for candidate effort, while unrestricted use can alter what assessment scores measure. The authors frame longer-term assessment design around fair, useful evaluation of human–AI collaboration rather than blanket prohibition.

## Key claims
- Candidate GenAI use can threaten construct-related validity when scores reflect GenAI literacy and access in addition to the intended candidate attributes.
- Differences in candidates' access to, willingness to use, and ability to use GenAI can create fairness and subgroup-difference concerns in selection outcomes.
- The FAIR framework distinguishes defensive responses (Forbid, Advise, Insulate) from Reimagine, which redesigns assessment around intended human–AI collaboration.
- Agentic AI and AI-enabled wearables may make conventional countermeasures, including digital proctoring and some face-to-face assessment practices, less reliable over time.
