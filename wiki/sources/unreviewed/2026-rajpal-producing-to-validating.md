# From Producing to Validating: How AI Is Deskilling Freelancers

**Citation.** Rajpal, N. (2026). *From Producing to Validating: How AI Is Deskilling Freelancers* (CHIWork ’26 position paper). arXiv:2608.26089. https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2608.26089

**Summary.** This position paper argues that generative AI can shift freelance knowledge work from producing original output toward validating and repairing AI-generated drafts. Using machine-translation post-editing and generated-code review as precedents, it proposes that lower-paid validation work can reduce opportunities for paid practice and compound deskilling among freelancers who lack employer-provided training and protections.

## Key claims
- The paper argues that clients increasingly retain freelancers to validate and correct generative-AI output, paying less than for original production even when detecting plausible errors requires substantial expertise.
- It proposes a compounding deskilling mechanism: market devaluation of original freelance work and survival-driven movement into validation work can reduce both earnings and opportunities to develop craft through paid practice.
- The paper argues that as model capability and end-to-end agents improve, validation roles may themselves become automation targets, leaving workers who were routed into repair work with less maintained or newly developed expertise.
- It recommends that platforms and clients compensate correction work for its expertise, provide skill-development infrastructure, and adopt disclosure norms that allocate accountability more clearly.

## Evidence & limitations
- This is a public arXiv position paper submitted to a CHIWork 2026 workshop, not a peer-reviewed empirical article; its central contribution is a reasoned synthesis and forward-looking argument.
- The argument draws on cited studies, historical comparison with machine-translation post-editing, and an ongoing interview study described as a manuscript in preparation; it reports no methods, sample, or results for that interview study.
- The public arXiv PDF was retrieved and matches the ingested Drive PDF exactly by SHA-256; the arXiv API was rate-limited during retrieval.
