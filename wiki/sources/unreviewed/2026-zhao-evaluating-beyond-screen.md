# Evaluating Beyond the Screen: Collective Assessment of AI-Generated Business Plans with Resource-Constrained Entrepreneurs

**Citation.** Zhao, Q., Pineda, M., Chhaya, K., Gautam, A., & Kotturi, Y. (2026). *Evaluating Beyond the Screen: Collective Assessment of AI-Generated Business Plans with Resource-Constrained Entrepreneurs*. arXiv:2608.16886.

**Summary.** This early-stage community-based participatory research study examines collective evaluation of AI-generated business plans among resource-constrained entrepreneurs. The authors extended BizChat with claim-to-input links and studied three community-program sessions in which participants evaluated plans through peer discussion and shared rubrics. The study argues that evaluation can be organized as a collective, community-level capacity rather than solely as individual on-screen verification.

## Key claims
- In three AI-and-entrepreneurship sessions with 14 workshop attendees, claim-to-input links made verification a visible comparison that peers could inspect and discuss.
- Paper rubrics and Think-Pair-Share activities supplied shared criteria for comparing generated business plans across participants.
- Follow-up interviews with two attendees suggest that evaluation can continue beyond workshops through printed plans and peer feedback, though the authors frame these as early findings.

## Evidence & limitations
- The study uses a community-based participatory research approach across three sessions and reports observations plus follow-up interviews with two attendees; it is an early-stage, small-sample preprint rather than a controlled outcome evaluation.
