# Work design

Work design concerns how the characteristics of work are organized and measured before any AI-specific redesign claim is made. [[2023-parker-smart-work-design]] develops SMART as a higher-order structure that groups established work characteristics into stimulating, mastery, autonomous, relational, and tolerable conditions.

The stimulating dimension comprises task and skill variety, information-processing demands, and problem-solving demands; mastery comprises feedback and role clarity; autonomous comprises decision, timing, and method autonomy; relational comprises social support, task significance, and beneficiary contact; and tolerable comprises low role overload, work–home conflict, and role conflict. [[2023-parker-smart-work-design]]

Across a 1,107-person study and a 709-person replication, the proposed five-factor SMART structure fit better than alternative higher-order models. [[2023-parker-smart-work-design]] In a three-wave sample of 573 participants who completed all waves, baseline SMART dimensions predicted later job satisfaction directly or through challenge appraisal, work meaningfulness, relatedness-need fulfillment, and activated negative affect. [[2023-parker-smart-work-design]] In a separate 108-person sample, employee ratings of relevant SMART dimensions related to leader-rated work performance in the expected directions. [[2023-parker-smart-work-design]]

SMART is therefore a reusable architecture for specifying which work characteristics a redesign preserves, strengthens, trades off, or degrades, rather than treating job quality as one undifferentiated outcome. The evidence supports the framework's measurement structure and its associations with satisfaction and performance, but it does not establish that changing a SMART characteristic through an intervention causes those outcomes. [[2023-parker-smart-work-design]]

## Connections

- Relates to [[work-redesign]] because AI-specific redesign claims need a baseline account of the work characteristics that task reallocation, control rights, feedback, and accountability may change.
- Relates to [[ai-mediated-work-experience]] because SMART's autonomy, mastery, relational, stimulation, and tolerability conditions provide a structured way to examine how AI changes resources, demands, agency, learning, and social experience.
- Relates to [[construct-validity]] because SMART bounds broad work-characteristics language into a tested higher-order structure instead of relying on an undifferentiated label for job quality.

## Contradictions & open questions

- [[2023-parker-smart-work-design]] supports SMART as an organizing and measurement framework, but its confirmatory-factor, longitudinal mediation, and correlational performance evidence does not establish the causal effects of work-design interventions.
- The studies were largely composed of managers and professionals, with leader-rated performance assessed in a part-time MBA sample; it remains open whether the same higher-order structure and outcome pathways generalize across occupations, employment arrangements, and AI-mediated workflows. [[2023-parker-smart-work-design]]
