# Open questions

The cross-topic questions driving this research program. Each spans several topics; questions that belong to a single topic live on that topic's page under **Contradictions & open questions**. When a question narrows into one construct, it becomes a topic and leaves this list.

- **Automation, augmentation, or protection.** Which tasks are candidates for automation vs. augmentation vs. protected human work — how is that decided, and where do worker preferences and moral boundaries diverge from technical capability? → [[automation-and-substitution]], [[human-ai-task-allocation]], [[human-ai-task-taxonomy]], [[moral-boundaries-of-ai-automation]], [[agentic-delegation]]
- **What makes adoption durable.** Which I-O constructs and change mechanisms best explain sustained, well-calibrated adoption of AI at work — beyond initial access or individual experimentation? → [[ai-adoption]], [[task-level-ai-adoption]], [[ai-receptivity]], [[ai-literacy]], [[ai-use-image-concerns]], [[employee-change-support]], [[behavioral-human-centered-ai]]
- **Measuring impact.** What does good measurement of AI workforce impact look like beyond productivity anecdotes? → [[ai-workforce-impact-measurement]], [[ai-agent-benchmark-validity]], [[construct-validity]], [[job-analysis]]
- **Measuring capability.** How should organizations measure human capital, AI capability, and combined human-AI capability without collapsing distinct constructs into one performance-relevant but uninterpretable index? → [[human-capital-resource-measurement]], [[competency-modeling]], [[construct-validity]]
- **The effort paradox.** When does improving AI quality reduce human effort, attention, or practice enough that oversight, learning, and expertise maintenance suffer — and which designs restore judgment without wasting effort? → [[automation-complacency]], [[cognitive-surrender]], [[cognitive-debt]], [[ai-induced-skill-erosion]], [[ai-mediated-learning]], [[cognitive-load-in-ai-assisted-work]]
- **Work experience under AI.** When does AI-mediated work improve versus degrade autonomy, competence, relatedness, identity, and felt job quality? → [[ai-mediated-work-experience]], [[digital-work-strain]], [[ai-mediated-choice-and-identity]], [[ai-enabled-job-crafting]]
- **Construct validity in AI-mediated assessment.** What does construct validity require in AI-mediated assessment and people analytics — and when does governance substitute for it rather than complement it? → [[construct-validity]], [[algorithmic-assessment]], [[evidence-based-management]], [[responsible-ai-deployment]]
- **Redefining work.** How does AI reshape job analysis and the definition of work itself, before HR systems are built on it? → [[job-analysis]], [[work-redesign]], [[competency-modeling]], [[human-ai-task-taxonomy]]
- **From experiments to organization.** Which organizational-learning and managerial capabilities turn AI experimentation into redesigned workflows and durable organizational knowledge rather than disconnected pilots? → [[organizational-learning-capability]], [[ai-workforce-transformation-implementation]], [[ai-mediated-organizational-networks]], [[ai-mediated-teamwork]]
- **Responsible deployment under uncertainty.** What evidence, governance, stakeholder voice, and reversal mechanisms make workforce-facing AI deployment responsible when validation evidence is incomplete but deployment pressure is real? → [[responsible-ai-deployment]], [[algorithmic-assessment]], [[moral-boundaries-of-ai-automation]]
